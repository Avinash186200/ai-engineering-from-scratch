# Glossary: mutual fund basics

## NAV
Net Asset Value is the price of one unit of a mutual fund. It equals the fund's total assets minus liabilities, divided by the number of units. NAV is published once per business day after the market closes.

## AUM
Assets Under Management is the total money a fund manages, usually quoted in crore rupees. A large AUM is not a sign of good returns by itself.

## Units
When you invest, you buy units at the day's NAV. Investing 10,000 rupees at a NAV of 50 gives you 200 units.
