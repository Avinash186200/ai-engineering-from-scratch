# Factsheet: Deccan Bond Fund (synthetic)

## Overview
Category: debt. Launched 2014. Average maturity: 3.2 years. The fund holds government
securities and high rated corporate bonds. It suits investors who want lower volatility
than equity.

## Charges
Exit load: 0.25% if redeemed within 30 days. Expense ratio: 0.45% (direct plan).
These are the charges of Deccan Bond Fund.

## How to invest
Minimum SIP: Rs 1,000 per month.
