# Factsheet: Himalaya Small Cap Fund (synthetic)

## Overview
Category: small cap equity. Launched 2017. Small cap funds can fall 30% or more in a bad
year. Recommended holding period: hold this small cap fund for at least seven years.

## Charges
Expense ratio: 1.60% (regular plan). Exit load: 1% if redeemed within one year.
These are the charges of Himalaya Small Cap Fund.

## How to invest
Minimum SIP: Rs 100 per month.
