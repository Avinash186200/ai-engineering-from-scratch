# Glossary: risk and return

## Volatility
Volatility measures how much a fund's daily returns swing. It is usually the annualised standard deviation of daily returns. Small cap funds are more volatile than debt funds.

## Momentum
Momentum is the recent return of a fund, for example over three months. Funds with strong momentum sometimes keep rising for a while, but momentum can reverse.

## Benchmark
A benchmark is the index a fund is compared against, such as a large cap index for a large cap fund.
