# Glossary: costs and charges

## Expense ratio
The expense ratio is the yearly fee a fund charges, as a percentage of your investment. It is deducted daily from the NAV, so you never see a bill. Direct plans have a lower expense ratio than regular plans because no distributor commission is paid.

## Exit load
An exit load is a fee charged when you redeem units before a minimum holding period, for example 1% if you sell within one year. Liquid funds often have a small exit load for the first seven days.

## Stamp duty
A small stamp duty of 0.005% applies to purchases of mutual fund units.
