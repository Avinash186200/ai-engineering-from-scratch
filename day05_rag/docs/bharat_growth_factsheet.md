# Factsheet: Bharat Growth Fund (synthetic)

## Overview
Category: large cap equity. Launched 2009. Fund manager: R. Iyer (fictional).
Benchmark: a large cap index. The fund invests mainly in the 100 largest listed companies,
with a tilt to banks and consumer companies.

## Charges
Expense ratio: 0.85% (direct plan). Exit load: 1% if redeemed within 365 days.
These are the charges of Bharat Growth Fund.

## How to invest
Minimum SIP: Rs 500 per month. Minimum lump sum: Rs 5,000.
