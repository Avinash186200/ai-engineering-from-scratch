# Glossary: ways to invest

## SIP
A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount every month on a chosen date. SIPs average the purchase price over time, which is called rupee cost averaging.

## Lump sum
A lump sum is a single one-time investment of a large amount.

## SWP
A Systematic Withdrawal Plan redeems a fixed amount every month, often used for regular income in retirement.

## ELSS
Equity Linked Savings Schemes are tax-saving equity funds with a three-year lock-in period.
